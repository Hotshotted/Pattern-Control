Privacy policy

Pattern Work · effective 2 November 2026

In short: Pattern Work collects no personal data.

  • There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to.
  • There are no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • Your progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not read an advertising identifier. We do not receive any information about you or how you play.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, stars, upgrades, unlocked airports and cosmetics, goals and settings such as the volume) in the app’s own storage on your device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app (uninstalling it on iPhone, or clearing the site data if you play in a browser) deletes it.

No third parties

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party software that sends information anywhere, and the app itself makes no network requests. The “Watch to double” and “Refund fine” buttons only play a short placeholder screen; no ad is loaded.

Playing in a browser

If you play the web version, the page is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches the game, and may keep them in its server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not receive, read or use those logs. The game does not use cookies.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes (for example if we add analytics, accounts or advertising), we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and the store listing’s privacy answers will be updated to match.

# UI audit

Overlapping, clipped and hidden interface is a release blocker. `tools/ui_overlap_check.gd` opens every screen, panel, popup, tab and state of the game at six screen sizes and reports, in pairs, what overlaps what, what is clipped, what is off the screen or under the notch, and what cannot be tapped. CI runs it on every push and fails on anything new. This page is generated by `tools/ui_audit_report.py` from the two reports in `docs/ui_audit/`.

## The airport detail screen: what covered what, and why

The screen with *Fly here* drew everything itself, each thing at its own hard-coded offset from the top of the card (`y0 = 0.667 x height`), and the *Fly here* button at a fixed distance from the bottom. Nothing owned the space, so later additions landed on top of earlier ones:

* **No engines (a chip at x = width - 370, y0 + 92 .. 144)** sat on the **mastery block** (rank line, bar and `N / N to rank N` at the same corner, y0 + 108 .. 154), added after it: the rank, its bar and the progress text were under the chip and could not be read or reached. This was the case on every airport with three stars (the picture of the old layout was removed with the other pictures; the numbers below come from `docs/ui_audit/before/report.json`).
* **Stars and Improve (two chips at y0 + 26 .. 78)** were fine alone, but the name and the tag were fitted to `width - 360` assuming those chips were all there was on the right, so on a long name the text ran under them, and the chips were 52 units tall (15 pt on a phone: under half a tap).
* **The improvement chips (y0 + 150 .. 326)** on the Improve tab covered the mastery progress text (y0 + 154) of the same card.
* **`Hangar N, profit after N landings` (y0 + 372)** ran into the **Fly here** button on screens whose height moved the button up relative to the card: the text was anchored to the top, the button to the bottom.
* The improvement names were shrunk in a loop down to **11** units to fit their chips.

Why: a screen of hand-placed rectangles has no layout, only coordinates, and the only check was a person looking at one phone size. The fix is a layout: `scripts/DetailSheet.gd` builds the card from containers (`PanelContainer` > `MarginContainer` > `VBoxContainer` > a `ScrollContainer` with the content, and the *Fly here* chip pinned under it), so nothing can sit on anything else; what does not fit scrolls, and *Fly here* stays in view. Every size in it is a minimum (a label wraps, a chip grows to its words and to 44 pt), not a position.

## The allowlist

Intentional overlaps are allowed only through the `ALLOW` list at the top of `tools/ui_overlap_check.gd`, one entry per case, each with a `why` (fields: `screen` (a glob on the state name), `size` (optional glob), `type`, `a` and optionally `b` (substrings of the two element names), `why`). Currently it has **0** entries: every problem the tool found was fixed, none had to be allowed.

## What the tool does not open

Nothing is skipped silently: the tool prints this list with its totals.

* The privacy policy page: it opens the system browser (`web/privacy.html`); it is not an in-game screen.
* The TUNE panel and the perf overlay: developer tools behind `?tune` / `?perf` in the page address, never shown to players.
* Confirmation dialogs: the game has none (the shop's hold-to-buy preview sheet is covered, for every item).
* Popups: where one appears depends on where the aircraft is; they are checked at the screen's left, middle and right edges and with the longest texts.
* The boot splash (the studio mark and a bar while the game pre-warms) is drawn by a class of its own at fixed sizes, centred: it is checked by measuring (the wordmark is 633 units wide on a screen that is never narrower than 1080).

## What is checked, and how

* **Overlap**: two visible things (text, labels, icons, buttons) whose rectangles overlap, unless one is nested in the other (a label inside *all of* its own button). Text is measured by its ink (cap height to descender), canvas-drawn text and icons are recorded as they are drawn (`Art._ds`, `_dms`, `glyph`), Controls are read from the tree (clipped by their Scroll). Two buttons whose *tap areas* overlap are reported as `covered`.
* **Clipped**: text wider than the width it is drawn with, a wrapped run that lost its last line, text that leaves the button or panel it is on, a word broken across lines.
* **Off-screen / safe-area**: any part outside the screen, or text and buttons under the notch, the Dynamic Island or the home indicator (the safe area of each size is in the tool).
* **Tap size**: a button's tap area under 44 x 44 points (`Style.px_per_pt` units per point), or covered by another control.
* **Small text**: under the floor of its role: caption 11 pt, body 15 pt, button label 17 pt on a 390-pt phone (31, 42, 48 units); headings as designed; or a size the pre-warm does not rasterise. See `docs/style.md`, *Layout and legibility rules*.
* Every state is also run with the largest numbers (9,999,999 coins, an eight-digit payout) and the longest real strings (the longest toast, achievement and mastery lines).
